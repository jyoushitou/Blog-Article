## GET

### 传来的JSON

```http
GET /api/articles?page=1&pageSize=10&tag=vue&keyword=%E5%85%A5%E9%97%A8 HTTP/1.1
Host: localhost:8080
Accept: application/json
```
- **`tag=vue`**：值就是 `vue`，不用解码
- **`keyword=%E5%85%A5%E9%97%A8`**：这里是实际传来的参数，中文utf-8编码，每个%后面的16进制数为其的一字节
